# Unemployment Analysis with Python

## CodeAlpha Data Science Internship – Task 2

### Objective
Analyze unemployment data in India to understand unemployment trends, investigate the impact of COVID-19, identify regional patterns, and derive meaningful insights.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab

## Analysis Performed
- Data cleaning and preprocessing
- Unemployment rate distribution
- Monthly unemployment trends
- COVID-19 impact analysis
- Rural vs Urban comparison
- Region-wise unemployment analysis
- Top and Bottom 10 regions
- COVID-19 regional impact
- Correlation analysis
- Data visualization

## Key Insights
- Unemployment increased sharply during the COVID-19 period.
- The average unemployment rate increased from approximately 9.51% before March 2020 to 17.77% from March 2020 onward.
- Urban areas had a higher average unemployment rate than rural areas.
- Tripura had the highest average unemployment rate among the regions analyzed.
- Meghalaya had the lowest average unemployment rate.
- Puducherry showed the highest increase in unemployment during the COVID-19 period.

## Conclusion
The analysis shows that COVID-19 had a significant impact on unemployment, with considerable variation across regions and between rural and urban areas.
